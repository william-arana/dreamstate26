What Live timing does

  • On now cards show who's playing on each stage, minutes left and who's next. Tap one to hear their tracks.
  • Red line marks the current time on the grid.
  • LIVE badges count down each set with a progress bar. Finished sets fade out.
  • Your ★ picks stay highlighted so you can spot them fast.

It runs off your phone's clock in Pacific time, so it works offline too. It turns on by itself during the festival; tap Live timing to turn it off.

A fan-made companion for Dreamstate SoCal 2026. Hear the sets before you get there, plan your weekend, and find your way around the Queen Mary.

Important · Save it to your phone
📶
Works offline at the festival

Cell service dies in big crowds. Once it’s on your home screen, set times, your starred plan and the map open with no signal. Open it from there once before you go so everything’s saved.

⊕
Add to Home Screen

iPhone: in Safari tap Share (the square with the arrow) → Add to Home Screen.
Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
It opens full screen like an app, with its own icon. Star your sets in the home screen app, since iPhone keeps its picks separate from Safari’s.

♫
What needs a signal

Song previews stream, so they need a connection. Everything else is built to keep working.

Preview any track

Tap ▶ for a 30-second preview. The player stays docked at the bottom while you browse.

Build a queue

Tap the queue button to line songs up. Open the player to see what’s up next or remove songs.

⇄
Autoplay & shuffle

Turn on Autoplay to roll through a whole set hands-free. Shuffle mixes it up.

Hit Apple Music or Spotify on any track, or grab the full Friday, Saturday or both-days playlist up top.

3 · Plan
★
Star sets for your plan

On Set Times, tap ☆ on a set. My Plan lists your picks in order and flags any overlaps. Saved on your phone.

👥
Share your plan

Under My plan, add your name and tap Share my plan to text your picks to friends. Their link shows your sets with a dashed outline and which ones you’re both going to, and they can add them in one tap.

●
Live now

During the festival a red line shows the current time, and sets that are on now get a LIVE badge and progress bar.
